# Research

## Research Interests

- Nonlinear Control; Adaptive Control; Optimal & Robust Control; Nonparametric Control; RKHS Embedding Methods; Adaptive Dynamic Programming; Reinforcement Learning  
- Data-Driven Modeling; Koopman Operator Theory; Online Parametric and Nonparametric Estimation; Physics-Informed Machine Learning; Model Reduction  
- Optimization and Surrogate Modeling of High-Temperature Plasmas; Advanced Control of Magnetically Confined Plasmas; AI-Driven Plasma Solutions  

## Research Experience

### Experimental Campaigns at Major International Fusion Research Facilities

- **Proximity Control and Safe Reinforcement Learning Algorithms for Plasma Regulation with Stability Limit Avoidance**, KSTAR (Korea Superconducting Tokamak Advanced Research), Korea Institute of Fusion Energy (KFE), Daejeon, South Korea (March 2026)  
  - Led the development and integration of the safe reinforcement learning category in the KSTAR plasma control system (PCS).  
  - Collaborated with GA and KFE specialists on proximity control and plasma density regulation.  
  - Led the experimental evaluation of reference governor and safe reinforcement learning algorithms for plasma regulation with density-limit avoidance in KSTAR.  

- **Adaptive Density Regulation for ITER and Future Reactors by Simultaneous Gas Puffing and Pellet Injection**, KSTAR (Korea Superconducting Tokamak Advanced Research), Korea Institute of Fusion Energy (KFE), Daejeon, South Korea (March 2026)  
  - Participated in collaborative KSTAR experiments on real-time plasma density control using a controller framework adapted from prior DIII-D studies.  
  - Provided technical guidance on modifications to the underlying control code during its porting to the KSTAR plasma control system.  

- **Optimal Actuator Allocation for Integrated Control**, TCV, Swiss Plasma Center, École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne, Switzerland (December 2025)  
  - Provided technical and algorithmic guidance for the implementation of an actuator allocation algorithm in the TCV plasma control system in collaboration with the Swiss Plasma Center team and ITER researchers.  
  - Participated in experimental validation of the allocation algorithm across multiple plasma control test cases.  

- **Advanced Density Control in DIII-D for ITER and Beyond**, DIII-D National Fusion Facility, General Atomics, San Diego, CA, USA (April 2025)  
  - Guided the development of a model reference adaptive plasma density controller incorporating gas-puffing delays.  
  - Provided theoretical oversight for the controller design.  
  - Participated in collaborative experiments demonstrating adaptive plasma-density regulation using pellet injection and delayed gas puffing.  

- **Advanced Density Regulation for ITER and Future Reactors by Simultaneous Gas Puffing and Pellet Injection**, KSTAR (Korea Superconducting Tokamak Advanced Research), Korea Institute of Fusion Energy (KFE), Daejeon, South Korea (January 2025)  
  - Assisted in the development and validation of KSTAR plasma control system (PCS) infrastructure for mimicking ITER-like delayed gas puffing.  
  - Collaborated with KFE specialists on plasma control and pellet injection systems.
